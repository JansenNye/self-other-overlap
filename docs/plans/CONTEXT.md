Those notebooks are not released here. They mixed running interpretation and future
work; they are not evidence of pre-run decisions. Their old links now lead here rather
than to missing files. The actual captured decision rules are in the linked run plans.

the final prompt token, then an earlier content token, then the Mistral and Gemma
follow-ups. These have separate captured versions of `ADAPTER_ROUND.md` in the index.
The later localization, bisection, constant replacement, and offset plans follow those
measurements. See the [experiment map](../EXPERIMENTS.md) for their correspondence to
the published post. References between plans point to the version index, so a later
addendum is not silently substituted for an earlier plan.
